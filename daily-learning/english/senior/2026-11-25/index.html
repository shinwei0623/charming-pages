校園烹飪日

Cooking Day at School · 主題：food

Every year, our school holds an outdoor cooking event to honor local tradition. My team decided to make a healthy fruit salad. The key to our recipe was finding the right balance between sweet and sour flavors. We carefully picked fresh apples, berries, and honey as our main ingredient list. After finishing our dish, we sat near the playground to enjoy our meal. We looked out at the distant horizon, admiring the scenic view of the mountains behind our campus. Everyone cheered because cooking together made our school day super fun and memorable.

趣味週末藝術之旅

A Fun Weekend Art Trip · 主題：hobbies

Last weekend, Leo visited an interactive art exhibition with his best friends. He felt very enthusiastic about trying all the different hands-on activities. Being a curious teenager, he spent hours exploring how light and music mixed together. In one room, he tapped on digital drums, following a fast drum rhythm that made his heart race. After spending hours at the show, they went to a nearby night market. There, they enjoyed delicious Taiwanese cuisine, like stinky tofu and boba tea. Leo took many photos and shared them online, declaring it the best weekend trip ever.
