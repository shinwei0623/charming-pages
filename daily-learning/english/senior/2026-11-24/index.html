星期三的彩排

Wednesday Rehearsal · 主題：music

Every Wednesday, the school band stays after class for rehearsal. Dana tunes her guitar and listens for the melody of their new song. The drums start softly, then the whole group joins in. They will perform at the autumn fair, so every note matters. When they finish, a few teachers who stopped by clap like a small audience. Dana’s hands are tired, but the music still seems to inspire her. She writes one more line in her notebook: practice with friends can turn a simple tune into something they are proud to share.

步道上的晨光

Morning on the Trail · 主題：nature

At dawn, the photography club walks a quiet trail above the town. Alex lifts his camera to capture the first light on the hills. The landscape changes quickly as the fog lifts, so he needs patience. He waits, checks the composition, and only then presses the shutter. His teacher reminds them to appreciate not only the perfect shot, but also the slow morning itself. On the way down, they compare photos and laugh at a blurry bird. Alex decides to come back next week—the trail will be the same, but the light never is.
