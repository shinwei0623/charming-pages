The Big Musical Challenge · 主題：music

Hey guys! Our school music club is facing a huge challenge. We have to write an original song for the upcoming festival. First, we need to finish a piano composition that blends modern pop with our cultural heritage. It is not easy, but we decided to collaborate instead of working alone. Every afternoon after class, we rehearse in the old music room until our fingers hurt. Last night, Leo added a cool drum beat, and Mia wrote amazing lyrics. We are super tired, but we believe our hard work will pay off on stage next Friday!

濕地的一天

A Day at the Wetland · 主題：nature

Last weekend, our class went to the local wetland for a nature conservation project. We wanted to help protect the birds and local plants. As a volunteer, I helped clean up the trash near the water. Later, our teacher showed us how to observe migrating birds through binoculars. It was amazing to see them so close! We also discussed some smart innovation ideas for saving water in our daily lives. The peaceful atmosphere of the wetland made everyone feel relaxed and connected to nature. It was truly an unforgettable Saturday!
