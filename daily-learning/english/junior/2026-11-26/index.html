河邊的一天

A Day by the River · 主題：nature

Hi guys! Today was such a fun Saturday. I met my best friend Leo in the morning. Our favorite hobby is taking photos of nature. We walked all the way to the big river behind our school. Sometimes, people like to travel far away to see cool places, but our local nature is amazing too. Later, we visited a small local museum about old boats. We learned a lot about our town’s history. After that, we ate some delicious beef noodles at a nearby shop. It was a tiring but super happy weekend!
