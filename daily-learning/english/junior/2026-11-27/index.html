森林散步記

A Walk in the Woods · 主題：nature

Leo loves hiking on weekends with his family. Last week, they visited a green forest near their town. Leo’s father drove the car, but he left his phone on the table at home. Luckily, they did not get lost because their clever dog, Bobo, walked in front of them. Bobo is very famous in their neighborhood because he can find any path easily. On Sunday, they spent three hours walking under big trees and listening to sweet birds. Leo felt very happy and relaxed. He wants to visit this quiet park again next month with his best friends.

廚房特別驚喜

A Special Kitchen Surprise · 主題：food

Amy and her older sister wanted to cook dinner for their tired parents. They bought vegetables and rice from the market, but something went wrong while cooking. Amy accidentally used salt instead of sugar! Her sister is sixteen years old, so she quickly helped fix the soup with fresh water and juice. When their parents arrived home tonight, the food tasted surprisingly good. There were many warm smiles around the table. Amy learned that cooking with family is fun even when you make mistakes. They decided to cook another delicious meal together next weekend.
