A Musical Weekend Plan · 主題：music

Today, Leo and his classmates visited the local library after school. They wanted to find some books about the upcoming summer festival. The weather was warm and sunny, so everyone felt excited. Inside the quiet building, they read about traditional drums and songs. Later, Leo saw a colorful poster on the wall. It said there would be a free concert in the park this Saturday. Leo loves music, so he immediately called his best friend, Sam. They decided to meet at the park early to get good seats. It was going to be a fun weekend for them!

艾咪最愛的星期六

Amy's Favorite Saturday · 主題：hobbies

Every Saturday morning, Amy likes to spend time outside in her family’s small garden. She drinks some fresh juice and helps water the green plants. After that, her mom invites her to help cook delicious morning food in the kitchen. They make eggs, toast, and fruit for breakfast. In the afternoon, the weather gets hot, so Amy goes to the local community pool to swim with her brother. They splash water and play happily together for two hours. Amy really enjoys her relaxed weekend with her family because she can do all her favorite activities.
