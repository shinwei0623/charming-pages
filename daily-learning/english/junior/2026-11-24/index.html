放學後的籃球

After-School Basketball · 主題：sports

After school, Mei runs to the gym with her team. They practice passing and shooting for one hour. At first, Mei cannot score, but her friends cheer for her. She tries again and again. Slowly, her skill gets better. When the last ball goes into the basket, everyone smiles. “See? You can do it,” her friend says. Mei feels happy and tired. They sit on the floor, drink water, and talk about Saturday’s game. Tomorrow they will practice again, and she already wants to help her team win.

市場裡的點心

A Snack at the Market · 主題：food

On Saturday morning, Lin goes to the market with his mom. The air smells sweet and warm. They buy fruit, bread, and a small snack to eat on the way. Lin is hungry, so he tries a hot onion pancake. “This is delicious!” he says, and they share it on a bench. His mom laughs and wipes the sauce from his chin. A little cat walks by and sits near their bags. Before they leave, Lin thanks the cook and looks back at the busy stalls. He thinks the best part of the market is not only the food, but the time they share together.
